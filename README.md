## How I work

1. **Security first, delivery always.** Controls should reduce risk without stopping useful work.
2. **Humans approve the critical steps.** Automation doesn't remove accountability.
3. **Deterministic first.** Scripts and CI do the executing; AI researches, drafts and reviews.
4. **Evidence over aspiration.** Governance should leave an audit trail.
5. **Small, controlled changes** over large, fragile programmes.
